cold room story — for Dec newsletter

what happened (pieced together from Marco, Shauna, Dev + the alarm log)

- night of Tues 7 Oct, Brackenby depot
- cold room 2 compressor failed. alarm log says temp alarm 01:38. Marco says he heard it "about 1:40" so that matches
- inside was ~14 pallets — mostly dairy (yogurts, cream, some cheese) + 2 pallets of fresh pasta for the Kendrick's order going out at 6am
- room temp was 3°C, rising. dairy has to stay under 5°C or we have to bin it (food safety rule, not our choice). Shauna checked — it hit 4.1°C by about 2:10
- only 4 people on night shift: Marco (shift lead), Shauna, Dev, and Pete (agency, 2nd week!)
- maintenance on-call (Gary) rang back at ~1:55, said he couldn't get there before 4 at the earliest — he was at the Lowfield site
- cold room 1 was full. no space
- Marco remembered the bakery supplier next door (Aldwick Bakes) runs a night shift + has a big chiller. walked over and knocked on their loading door. their supervisor — Ines I think? Marco's not 100% on the name, check before printing — said yes they had space for "maybe 10 pallets"
- they moved 10 pallets across the yard with 2 pallet trucks. no forklift bc the yard is uneven (Dev's point). took about 40 min
- other 4 pallets they squeezed into cold room 1 by restacking — Shauna's idea. Marco said it was "like tetris"
- Pete did the paperwork for the transfer the whole time, every pallet logged with temp. Marco says his log was "better than mine"
- all product stayed under 5°C. Shauna has the readings
- Gary arrived 4:20, fixed compressor (failed start capacitor — about a £30 part apparently)
- pallets moved back about 5:30. Kendrick's order left 6:05 — 5 min late
- est value of product saved: ~£38k per Finance (Lorraine). DON'T say "saved the company" — Lorraine said the insurance would've covered most of it, just with a big excess + hassle
- Aldwick Bakes didn't want anything. we sent them a box of cheese + a thank-you card signed by the whole depot
- Marco doesn't want to be made out as a hero. he said "it was all four of us"
- Pete has since been offered a permanent contract (started 3 Nov) — not because of this, was already in process, but he's happy for us to mention it
- photo: Shauna has one of the pallet trucks in the yard at 3am, bit blurry
