**INTERNAL — NOT FOR CUSTOMER DISTRIBUTION**

**To:** Member Services, Front Desk Leads, Regional Ops
**From:** Membership Operations & Commercial Policy
**Re:** Rationalisation of Membership Hold Entitlements (Policy MS-17 rev. 4)
**Effective:** 1 January 2027

**1. Background**

Following the H1 utilisation review, the current hold (freeze) entitlement structure has been identified as a driver of revenue leakage and operational overhead. Under MS-17 rev. 3, all tiers are eligible for up to 12 weeks of hold per membership year at nil cost, in increments of no less than 7 days, with no documentation requirement. Analysis indicates that 31% of holds are initiated within 14 days of a scheduled price adjustment and that hold-to-cancellation conversion is 2.4x the baseline churn rate.

**2. Revised entitlement framework**

2.1 *Standard and Off-Peak tiers:* Hold entitlement is reduced to a maximum of 8 weeks per membership year. A hold administration charge of £5.00 per calendar month (pro-rated, minimum one month) will apply for the duration of the hold.

2.2 *Premium tier:* Retains 12-week entitlement at nil cost. No change.

2.3 *Medical and maternity holds:* Exempt from the administration charge and from the 8-week cap, subject to submission of supporting documentation (GP letter, fit note or MAT B1 or equivalent) within 14 days of hold initiation. Absent documentation, the hold will be reclassified as standard and charged accordingly.

2.4 Minimum hold increment is increased from 7 days to 1 calendar month.

2.5 Holds must be requested no later than 5 working days prior to the next billing date. Requests received after this cutoff will take effect from the following billing cycle.

**3. Transitional arrangements**

Holds active as at 31 December 2026 will continue under rev. 3 terms until their scheduled end date. Holds requested on or after 1 January 2027 fall under rev. 4. Any member who has already used more than 8 weeks of hold in the current membership year will not be eligible for further standard holds until their membership anniversary.

**4. Channel and comms**

Members to be notified via email no later than 1 December 2026 (30-day notice requirement per T&Cs cl. 9.2). Front desk scripting to be issued w/c 24 November. App hold flow to be updated by Digital (ticket DIG-2291).

**5. Escalation**

Retention-risk exceptions may be authorised at Club Manager discretion up to a maximum of 2 per club per month, logged in CRM under reason code HX-RET.
