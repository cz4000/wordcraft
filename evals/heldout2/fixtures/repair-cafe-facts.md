Kestrel Lane Repair Café — facts for the appeal (from Deb, coordinator)

- started Nov 2021, me + 4 others. now 23 volunteers on the list but really about 12 regulars
- meet 2nd Saturday of every month 10am–1pm
- people bring broken stuff, we try to fix it with them (not for them — we show them how). free, donations welcome (tin on the table, usually £60–£90 a session)
- since we started: 1,140 items brought in (Pete's spreadsheet), about 68% fixed or partly fixed. most common: lamps, toasters/kettles, clothes (zips!!), bikes, laptops (slow ones mostly), kids' toys
- Pete reckons that's about 2.3 tonnes of stuff kept out of landfill but honestly that's a guess based on average weights so maybe don't make a big deal of the number

THE PROBLEM:
- St Barnabas hall (where we've been since the start, they let us have it free) is closing for refurb from January. could be 18 months. maybe longer, the vicar said "at least a year"
- Kestrel Lane Community Centre will have us, same Saturday slot, but it's £45 a session for the room + we'd need to pay for our own public liability insurance which the church covered before (quote £380/yr)
- also our toolkit is basically people's own tools. Want to buy a proper shared kit, soldering station, PAT tester etc — roughly £1,100
- so for the first year: room 12 x £45 = £540, insurance £380, tools £1,100, plus a bit for consumables (fuses, thread, glue, bike inner tubes) say £300. = £2,320. call it £2,400

VOLUNTEERS — this is actually the bigger worry
- 2 of our 3 electrical people are stepping back (Keith is moving, Sandra's knees). Electricals are half of what comes in
- we need: anyone handy with electrics (we'd train on PAT testing — the new tester would let us do it properly), sewing/mending, bikes. ALSO people to just do the welcome desk, make tea, keep the sign in book. you don't need to be able to fix anything
- even once every 2–3 months is useful

how to help:
- donate: on our page, or tin at the café, or bank transfer (details on the page — don't put account no. in the newsletter, Pete says)
- volunteer: email kestrelrepair@example.org or just come along on Sat 8 Nov (last one at St Barnabas!) and say hi
- spread the word

nice stories if useful:
