## About us

Northgate Adult Learning Trust has been running courses for adults in Northgate and the surrounding villages since 1987. Every year around 2,300 learners join us for anything from English for speakers of other languages (ESOL) and functional maths to upholstery, digital skills for beginners and our hugely popular Return to Work programme. Most of our courses run in the evenings and at weekends and are taught by a team of around 45 sessional tutors.

We are a small charity with a big reach. We are proud that over half our learners have no qualifications above GCSE level, and we work hard to keep our courses affordable or free.

## The role

We are looking for an organised, people-focused Programme Coordinator to keep our term-time programme running smoothly. You will be the main point of contact for our tutors and the first port of call for learners with questions about their course.

Key responsibilities:

- Build the termly timetable of around 120 courses across three venues, working with the Head of Programmes
- Recruit, induct and support sessional tutors, including sorting out cover when a tutor is unavailable (often at short notice)
- Manage enrolments using our learner database and deal with queries by phone, email and in person
- Make sure registers, learner records and funding paperwork are complete and accurate for our termly returns to the council and our funders
- Book rooms and equipment, and liaise with venue staff
- Help organise our two Open Days a year and our end of year celebration of learning
- Support the team with ad hoc projects

## About you

Essential:
- Experience of coordinating events, programmes, rotas or similar, with lots of moving parts
- Confident with spreadsheets and databases (our learner database is called LearnTrack — training given)
- Excellent organisational skills and attention to detail
- Calm under pressure and able to juggle competing priorities

Desirable:

- Experience of working with people from a wide range of backgrounds, including people for whom English is not a first language
