# Pangram scan, round 3 (Opus), 2026-10-25

Pangram's free web checker (model "Pangram 4.1"), run by hand in a browser. The free tier allows 20 credits at 1 credit per 100 words, which covered 6 texts. Each text is a whole reply as Claude gave it, rep 1, including any lead-in or note after a draft.

| Task | With the skill | Without the skill |
|---|---|---|
| Announcement | 100% AI (157 words) | 100% AI (260 words) |
| Reply email | 100% AI (157 words) | 100% AI (221 words) |
| Decline email | 100% AI (154 words) | 100% AI (236 words) |

**Reading:** the skill does not make Claude's writing read as human to Pangram, and nothing here suggests it should. The design treats Pangram as a reported measure, not a pass condition, because it detects whether a model wrote the text, not whether the text reads well. Six texts is a small sample.
