WHEN DO I GET MY POTS
- people ask this CONSTANTLY
- 3-4 weeks. it has to dry slowly, bisque fire, glaze, glaze fire. can't rush it or it cracks
- we email you when they're ready. collect any time in opening hours
- if not collected in 3 months we'll email twice and then donate them to the charity shop / give them away. (we've got shelves of stuff from 2024 lol)
- postage? not really, too fragile. if you're desperate we can, £8 and it's at your own risk

WHAT TO WEAR
