To whom it may concern,

I am writing to apply for the role of Visitor Experience Assistant at Calderwood Wildlife Park which I saw advertised on your website. I am a hard working, passionate and enthusiastic person and I believe I would be a great fit for your team.

Ever since I was a little girl I have loved animals. My family always had pets including two dogs, a rabbit called Biscuit and at one point some stick insects. I used to watch every nature documentary on TV and I wanted to be a vet for a long time, although I have since realised that my strengths are more in working with people than in science. I have visited Calderwood many times with my family and I have always loved the atmosphere and the way that the keepers talk to the visitors.

For the past two years I have worked part time at The Copper Pot café in Pellbury town centre while finishing my A levels (Biology, English Language and Geography, results B, B, C). In this role I serve customers, work the till, and deal with complaints. On one occasion a young boy got separated from his grandma in the market and came into the café very upset. I sat with him, got him a drink and kept him calm while my manager called the market office, and they were reunited after about twenty minutes. My manager said I handled it really well and that is something I am proud of.

I also volunteer every other Sunday at Hollybank Hedgehog Rescue, where I clean out enclosures, prepare food and weigh the hedgehogs and record it on the spreadsheet. I have learnt a lot about animal welfare and also about talking to members of the public who bring in hedgehogs they have found, who are often worried or upset. Last autumn I helped run our stall at the Pellbury Show and we raised £340.

I think I would be a great asset to Thornby Zoo because I am friendly, reliable and I am not afraid of hard work. I am a quick learner and I work well both in a team and on my own initiative. I have excellent communication skills and I am good at problem solving.

I am available to start immediatly and I am flexible about working weekends and school holidays, which I know is when the park is busiest. I have a full clean driving licence.

Thank you for taking the time to read my application. I hope to hear from you soon.
