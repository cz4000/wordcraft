Courier letter – bus cuts

Courier letters page says: "please keep letters under 500 words and include your full name and area. we may edit for length." email letters@dunmerecourier.co.uk
Consultation closes 14 Nov

FACTS (from the council consultation pack, p.6-9)
- proposal: withdraw all journeys on routes 47 and 47A after 19:00 Mon–Sat, and all Sunday services on the 47A
- "saving" £182,000 a yr (pack says £182k on p.6, the press release said "around £200,000")
- they say evening journeys average 9 passengers. (per journey? per hour? doesn't say!)
- 47 = Hospital – town centre – Brookfield – Carr Hill. 47A = same but via Stennet Park retail park
- "alternative": the council says people can use Dial-a-Ride. but Dial-a-Ride has to be booked 24 hrs ahead and stops at 6pm!!! (checked their leaflet)
- equality impact assessment says "low impact" ??

ME
- care assistant at Dunmere General, 12 yrs. late shift ends 21:30. I get the 21:45 47 home to Carr Hill
- I don't drive. taxi would be £14-16 each way. 4 lates a week = maybe £250/month. I'm not on a lot more than that a week
- loads of us on the 21:45 — I counted last Tues, 14 people got on at the hospital stop, most of them staff in uniform
- also Sat nights: kids coming back from the cinema at Stennet Park, the supermarket workers finishing at 10
- my neighbour Mr Faulkner (87) goes to his wife's care home in Brookfield every evening after his tea, gets the 18:50 there and comes back on the 20:10. without that he wouldn't see her in the evenings. he wouldn't write to the paper himself but said I can mention him (not full name — he said "say Ted, that's fine")

WHAT I THINK / ANGRY
- they're cutting the bus that gets the hospital staff home and then they'll wonder why they can't recruit
- "9 passengers" is meaningless without saying when. the 19:15 might be quiet, the 21:45 is full
- the council spent £400k? on the Market Square "public realm" (planters!) last year — careful, check figure, I think it was £420k. maybe don't put it in if I can't check
- not against saving money. could they run a reduced evening service — hourly instead of every 30 min? or just the last 2 journeys that match shift changes?
- has anyone asked the hospital trust to chip in? they subsidise staff parking, why not the bus
- want people to respond to the consultation – online at dunmere.gov.uk/haveyoursay or paper form at any library
- sign off: Donna Reece, Carr Hill
