match notes - Sun 25 Oct - Kellsby Town Women v Ridgefield Rovers Ladies
Vale Women's League Div 1, home (Moss Lane)

FT 3-2 US!!!

- first win v Ridgefield in 3 seasons (I think? Jo reckons we beat them in the cup in 2023 so maybe first LEAGUE win)

- squad of 14, Abbie out (ankle), Shaz back from holiday
