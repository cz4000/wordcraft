## 3. Our understanding of your requirements

Based on our site visit and the information provided by your Practice Manager, we understand your current environment as follows:

- **Sites:** three practices (Hollybank Road, Ferrywell and Orchard Street), each with its own broadband connection.
- **Users:** 36 staff who use a computer, including dentists, dental nurses, hygienists, reception and administration staff.
- **Devices:** approximately 38 workstations, including 9 surgery PCs connected to intraoral X-ray sensors, plus 4 laptops used by the partners and the Practice Manager.
- **Servers:** one on-premise server at Hollybank Road hosting the DentaFile practice management database; a second, older server at Ferrywell running the ClearView Imaging software. The Ferrywell server's operating system reaches the end of manufacturer support in 2027.
- **Backups:** currently performed nightly to an external USB drive, which the Practice Manager takes home on Fridays. In our view this presents a significant risk to the business.
- **Email:** a mixture of accounts on a cloud email and office suite and older mailboxes still hosted with a domain registrar.
- **Connectivity:** Orchard Street experiences frequent internet drop-outs, which staff report affect the booking system several times a month.
- **Current support:** provided by an independent consultant, who has advised that he will retire at the end of March 2027.

Your stated priorities were: reliable support when something goes wrong, not having to "chase one person", proper backups, and peace of mind on data protection.

## 4. Proposed services

We offer two tiers of managed service. Both include our core monitoring and helpdesk platform.

### 4.1 Essentials

- Unlimited remote helpdesk support, 08:00–18:00 Monday to Friday (excluding bank holidays)
- 24/7 automated monitoring of workstations and servers, with alerts to our service desk
- Monthly security patching of operating systems and common applications
- Managed antivirus and email filtering
- Asset register and software licence tracking
- Quarterly service review call

### 4.2 Complete (recommended)

Everything in Essentials, plus:

- One scheduled onsite day per month, rotating between your three sites
- Onsite attendance for Priority 1 and Priority 2 issues where remote resolution is not possible
- Managed cloud backup of both servers with 30-day retention (up to 2 TB included; additional storage chargeable)
- Annual cyber-awareness training for all staff (online modules and simulated phishing exercises)
- Out-of-hours emergency line for Priority 1 issues: 07:00–08:00 and 18:00–20:00 weekdays, and 08:00–13:00 Saturdays
- A named account manager
- A technology roadmap, reviewed with the partners every six months

### 4.3 Third-party and line-of-business applications

Support for DentaFile and ClearView Imaging will be provided on a **best-endeavours basis**. Kestrel Lane will log and chase faults with the software vendors on your behalf, but resolving faults within these applications remains the responsibility of the vendor and falls outside our service levels. Upgrades to these applications are treated as project work.

Your telephone system is supplied by a separate provider and is not included in this proposal.

## 5. Service levels

| Priority | Definition | Response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | A whole site is unable to work | 1 business hour | 4 business hours |
| P2 High | Several users affected, or one surgery unable to see patients | 2 business hours | 8 business hours |
| P3 Medium | One user affected, workaround available | 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| P4 Low | Requests, changes and "how do I" questions | 1 business day | 5 business days |

"Response" means a technician has acknowledged the ticket and begun work. Resolution times are targets, not guarantees. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 Monday to Friday. Where remote resolution is not possible, onsite attendance will be arranged for the next business day (Complete tier only).

**Service credits:** if we miss the P1 response target more than twice in a calendar month, you will receive a credit of 5% of that month's service fee. Credits are capped at 10% of the monthly fee and must be claimed in writing within 30 days.

**Exclusions:** the cost of replacement hardware; faults caused by changes made without our agreement; third-party applications (see 4.3); failures of internet connections supplied by third parties; and project work.

## 6. Onboarding and migration

We will move you onto our service in four phases over six to eight weeks:

1. **Weeks 1–2, discovery:** full documentation of your network, devices and accounts; installation of our monitoring agent; handover of passwords and licences from your current provider.
2. **Weeks 3–4, backup and email:** set-up of managed cloud backup; consolidation of all mailboxes onto the cloud email suite.
3. **Weeks 5–6, security:** applying our security baseline to all devices; replacing the firewall at each site; turning on multi-factor sign-in for all staff.
4. **Weeks 7–8, handover:** transfer to our service desk; a short introduction session for staff at each site.

**One-off onboarding fee:** £4,800 + VAT.

We recommend replacing the Ferrywell imaging server within the next 12 months. The indicative cost is £6,500–£8,000 including hardware and data migration. This will be quoted separately.

The client is responsible for securing the cooperation of the incumbent provider, including passwords, documentation and licence details. Delays caused by missing or incomplete information may be charged at our standard project rate.

## 8. Contract terms (summary)

- **Initial term:** 36 months from the service commencement date.
- **Renewal:** the agreement renews automatically for further 12-month periods unless either party gives at least 90 days' written notice before the end of the current term.
- **Price review:** fees may be reviewed annually on the anniversary of commencement by up to CPI plus 3%, capped at 9% in any one year.
- **Minimum billable users:** 40. User numbers may be increased at any time. Reductions take effect from the next anniversary and may not go below the minimum.
- **Early termination:** the client may end the agreement for convenience on 90 days' notice, subject to payment of 75% of the fees remaining for the unexpired term.
- **Termination for cause:** either party may terminate for a material breach not put right within 30 days of written notice.
- **Exit:** on termination, Kestrel Lane will hand over administrative passwords and documentation within 10 business days. Any further exit assistance is charged at project rates.
- **Liability:** capped at the fees paid in the previous 12 months.
- **Payment:** monthly in advance by direct debit. The onboarding fee is invoiced on signature.

The full terms are set out in our Master Services Agreement (version 7.2), supplied separately.

## 9. Security and data protection

We understand that as a dental group you hold special category health data about your patients, and that you remain the data controller for that information. Kestrel Lane will act as a data processor and will sign our standard data processing agreement with you.

- All backup data is stored in UK data centres and encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Kestrel Lane holds a recognised national cyber security certification, renewed annually, and is working towards a further information security standard by the end of 2027.
- All staff with access to client systems pass a background check before starting.
- We will enable multi-factor sign-in for all of your staff accounts during onboarding.
- In the event of a suspected data breach affecting your systems, we will notify you within 24 hours of becoming aware of it.
