You have been referred for the above procedure. Please read this letter and the enclosed information carefully as failure to follow the instructions may result in your procedure being cancelled or having to be repeated.

**CONFIRMING YOUR APPOINTMENT**
Please telephone the Bookings line on 01632 960 210 within 7 days of the date of this letter to confirm that you will attend. If we do not hear from you, your appointment may be offered to another patient and you may be referred back to your GP.

If you are unable to attend, please let us know as soon as possible so that the appointment can be offered to someone else. Patients who do not attend without informing us may be discharged in line with Trust Access Policy.

**MEDICATION**
Please bring a list of all your current medications with you, or the medicines themselves in their original packaging.

*Iron tablets* (e.g. ferrous sulphate, ferrous fumarate): stop taking these 7 days before your procedure.

*Constipating medications* (e.g. loperamide, codeine phosphate): stop 3 days before unless otherwise advised.

*Anticoagulant and antiplatelet medication* (including warfarin, apixaban, rivaroxaban, edoxaban, dabigatran, clopidogrel, prasugrel, ticagrelor): **do not stop these medicines unless advised.** You must contact the Pre-assessment nurse on 01632 960 233 as soon as possible and at least 7 days before your procedure. If you take warfarin you may need an INR blood test in the week before your procedure. Aspirin can usually be continued.

*Diabetes*: If you take tablets or insulin for diabetes, please refer to leaflet DBP/04 "Diabetes and bowel preparation" enclosed with this letter. If you have not received the leaflet please contact the unit.

All other medications should be taken as normal, but please note that any tablets taken within one hour of the bowel preparation may not be absorbed.

**BOWEL PREPARATION**
Your bowel preparation (2 sachets) has been posted separately / will be sent by the hospital pharmacy. If you have not received it 7 days before your procedure please contact the unit.

*Two days before your procedure (Tuesday 10 November):* begin the low residue diet (see enclosed diet sheet). Avoid high fibre foods including wholemeal bread, brown rice, fruit, vegetables, nuts, seeds and red meat. Suitable foods include white bread, white fish, chicken, eggs, plain biscuits and potatoes without skins.

*The day before your procedure (Wednesday 11 November):*
- Continue low residue diet. You may have a light breakfast and light lunch. Your lunch must be finished by 13:00.
- After 13:00 you must have **clear fluids only** (water, clear soup/broth, black tea or coffee, squash (not red or purple), clear apple juice, jelly (not red)).
- At 19:00 take the first sachet of bowel preparation as directed on the packet.

*The day of your procedure (Thursday 12 November):*
- At 07:00 take the second sachet of bowel preparation.
- You may continue to drink clear fluids until 12:30, after which you should have nothing further to drink.

The bowel preparation will cause frequent loose bowel movements. You should remain near a toilet. It is important to drink plenty of clear fluids to avoid dehydration.

**SEDATION AND GOING HOME**
Most patients are offered sedation and/or pain relief (Entonox gas). If you are having sedation you MUST arrange for a responsible adult to collect you from the unit and stay with you for 12 hours afterwards. You must not drive, operate machinery, drink alcohol or sign legal documents for 24 hours after sedation. **If you do not have an escort your procedure may be carried out without sedation or may be cancelled.** We are unable to release sedated patients to a taxi alone.

Your escort will be given a contact number to call to find out when you are ready for collection. Please be aware you may be in the unit for 2 to 4 hours. Emergency cases may take priority and this can cause delays.

**ON THE DAY**
Please bring: this letter, your list of medications, a dressing gown and slippers, and something to read. Please leave valuables at home. Please do not wear nail varnish or jewellery other than a wedding band.
