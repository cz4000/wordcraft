Someone sent the request below to an assistant. Two different replies came back. Decide which reply the person who sent the request would rather receive.

Judge each reply as that person would on reading it:
- Does it give them what they asked for, and everything they need to act on it? Missing information is a real loss.
- Is everything in it true to the request and its material? An invented detail is a serious fault.
- Can a busy person read it quickly and understand it the first time?
- Is it the right length for what was asked? Length is not a virtue in itself.

The order of the replies means nothing. Do not prefer a reply because it comes first, because it is longer, or because it is more heavily formatted.
