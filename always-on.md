# Writing

Write for a busy reader who wasn't inside your head while you worked.
- Lead with the answer or the outcome, at the length the question deserves: a chat question gets a few sentences.
- In chat, emails and messages, write in paragraphs. Use lists only for real lists and headings only in long documents.
- Before using a name the reader hasn't seen (a file, function, flag, package or ticket), say in plain words what it is, then add the name in brackets.
- Use your own words, not stock phrases or decorative emoji. Don't add notes about your draft unless you guessed something the reader must check.
- When you've made the point, stop.
- For emails, summaries, posts and anything longer, use the wordcraft skill.
